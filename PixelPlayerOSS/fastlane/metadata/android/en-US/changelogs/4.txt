Polylute 1.2.0-beta.1.

* Android 10 support, including SD card folders and music access.
* Library fills after adding your first folders, without restarting.
* Artist photos load for the music you open or play.
* Music info uses Find, Choose and Download. Reopening an unchanged catalog skips the refresh work.
* Pick your own genre images; backups keep them.
* Updated player layout, source shuffle and compact genre rows.
