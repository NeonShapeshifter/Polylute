Polylute 1.1.0.

* Five themes, and the whole app can take the colours of the album art. Immersive lyrics are back; the mini player's line can be dragged to seek.
* Optional music info from MusicBrainz and Wikimedia (off by default). Deezer is gone.
* New files in your folders are found. The Melody's EQ can be written, with your OK. Detailed logging in Maintenance.
* Better downsampling above 192 kHz, and an optional peak limiter, on the Android route.
* No location or exact-alarm permission.
